Club Socials

Members mark when they can play. Organisers pack fair courts and publish — fixtures appear in Upcoming with notifications.

Club Socials are availability-first sessions — not court booking software. You collect who’s free, arrange the courts, then publish into the same match flow everyone already uses. For rotating-partner nights, see Americano sessions.

Publish fair fixtures

See Court 1 and Court 2 pairings with balance deltas, swap players or reserves, then Publish to schedule. Everyone gets the matches — no spreadsheet round-robin.

Members see what’s on

Open Matches → Club Socials for sessions with sign-up counts, spaces and status. Past and upcoming socials stay in one list so the club rhythm is obvious.

Mark your availability

Pick the time slots you can play. Organisers see ratings in the sign-up list so packing stays fair. Share links can send members straight into the right session.

Formats clubs reuse

Save social formats — courts, slots, level ranges — so Thursday nights and Monday sessions don’t need reinventing every week.

Bring Padel Pals to your club

Download the app, select your club or add it, then invite members to join. Need help getting started? Contact us.
