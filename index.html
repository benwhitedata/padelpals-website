How to get started

Three steps from download to this week’s sessions.

1

Add your club

Get Padel Pals on iOS or Android. Open Profile, choose Your Club, then pick it from the list or add it if it is not listed yet.

2

Invite members

Players join the same club and mark when they can play. Socials, Americanos and leagues then share one membership list.

3

Run this week’s play

Publish socials, host Americano sessions and keep boxes moving — with fixtures, standings and member activity in one place.
