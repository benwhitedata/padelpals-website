Frequently asked questions

For club organisers

Download Padel Pals, open Profile, scroll to Your Club and tap Change. Search for the club or choose Add New if it is not listed. Members then pick the same club so socials, Americanos and leagues line up.

Either. Hosting Club Socials and Americano sessions, and playing as a member, works on iOS and Android.

No. Padel Pals organises who plays whom: availability, pairings, Americano sessions, box leagues, ratings and results. Court booking, membership fees and facility software stay with the club.

Open the Americano tab and create a session. Choose Play now or Sign-ups, how many players (adjustable), one or two courts, and Americano or Mexicano. You can set a rating band for who may sign up. Members join from the share link; guests are added by name and are not rated. Score in the app so the current round and standings stay live. Turn on Sync for share and live standings if you want a TV or club group to watch the board without signing in.
